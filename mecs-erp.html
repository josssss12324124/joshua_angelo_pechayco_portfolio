A collaborative internal enterprise resource planning system developed during my 500-hour internship at MECS Software Solutions. The system supports business operations through connected modules and structured workflows.

Overview

The MECS Internal ERP System brings together business functions such as Sales, CRM, Inventory, Purchasing, and related operational processes. My internship provided hands-on exposure to an existing business application, its data structures, task workflows, and collaborative development practices.

Development context

Work was performed within an existing codebase and team workflow. Tasks involved understanding requirements, reviewing flowcharts and database structures, implementing assigned changes, and coordinating source-code updates through GitHub.

Technical areas

  • CRUD features and business workflow implementation.
  • Database tables, relationships, seeders, and factories.
  • Frontend components using Vue.js and PrimeVue.
  • Backend development using Laravel and application validation.
  • Exposure to filtering, PDF/print output, and payment-related workflows.

What the system solves

The ERP organizes related business processes in one application rather than relying on disconnected manual records. The exact operational impact depends on the deployed modules and should not be quantified without measured evidence.
